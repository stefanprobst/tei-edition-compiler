This is the complete 73-document inventory at the pinned historical revision, with separately pinned registers. Ask a reader to attempt these tasks without coaching. Record success, hesitation, their wording, device and the exact URL. Developer/browser checks are not evidence of user-study success.

| Follow editorial commentary and return | Document 0001 | Do commentary markers, responsibility and backlinks make sense? |

Repeat image selection and commentary navigation on a phone. Try a direct document URL, reload and Back. Ask what they expected when the observed behavior surprises them. Prioritize failures that prevent reading or misrepresent editorial evidence before visual polish.

Record findings as: task / URL / observed behavior / reader expectation / severity / proposed change / retest result. No reader sessions have yet been recorded.

The manual `Edition preview` workflow compiles and verifies the locked corpus, packages only derived public files, and deploys that payload to GitHub Pages. Pushes do not automatically publish new editions. External images keep their original rights and service dependencies; image files and canonical XML are not included in the hosting payload.
